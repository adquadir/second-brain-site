Second Brain

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Second Brain uses your data only to run the app for you and your household; we never sell it, and you can delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and household details you enter.
  • Bank data: when you connect a bank through Plaid, we receive account names, balances, and transactions. We never see or store your bank username or password.
  • Content you add: tasks, lists, recipes, receipts, budgets, and calendar items.
  • Technical data: device type, app version, and error logs used to keep the app working.

How we use it

  • To show your finances, tasks, and plans in one place and keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To generate summaries and suggestions inside the app, if you turn on AI features.
  • To secure your account and fix problems.
  • To send sign-in codes and service messages. We do not send marketing email without your permission.

Plaid

We use Plaid to connect your bank accounts. By connecting, you allow Plaid to collect your financial data under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. You can disconnect a bank at any time in the app.

AI features

Some features use Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic: Ask, your daily brief, receipt, fridge and product scans, statement and text imports, email reading, and planning help. They are off until you turn them on in the app, which tells you what is sent before you agree. When they are on, Second Brain sends Anthropic only what the feature in use needs, such as the transactions behind a question or the photo you scan. Anthropic processes it to give the answer and does not use it to train its models. You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings; with them off, nothing is sent to Anthropic and the rest of the app keeps working. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share data only with:

  • Service providers that run the app for us (database hosting, bank connection, email delivery, error monitoring, calendar sync you choose to connect, and AI processing by Anthropic when you turn on AI features), each limited to what its service needs.
  • Members of your household, for the data you choose to share with them.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How we protect it

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Connecting a bank requires Face ID, fingerprint, or your device passcode. Access to our systems is limited and protected by multi-factor authentication.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your bank connections immediately and permanently delete your data within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data in the app, or request a copy of it by email.
  • Disconnect any bank at any time.
  • Delete your account and all your data inside the app.
  • Turn AI features off in Settings at any time.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.

To make a request, use the app or email acourdry@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children

Second Brain is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will notify you in the app before any material change to this policy takes effect. Questions: Abdulquadir Aderinto, acourdry@gmail.com.

Second Brain

Your money, tasks, calendar and food, in one place.

Second Brain is an iPhone app that brings the parts of running a life together and has an assistant think about them with you: what is due, what can wait, what your next paycheck has to cover, and what is in the fridge for dinner.

Money

A plan for each paycheck: bills, savings and what is safe to spend, with a forecast of where your balance is heading. Banks connect through Plaid.

Tasks

Lists, sections, reminders and a day view on your calendar. Ask how to do a task and get the steps.

Calendar

Your Google Calendar alongside your tasks, so free time is real free time.

Food

Groceries, meals and recipes, with what you already have counted first.

Availability

Second Brain is in private testing on iPhone and is not yet available to the public.
